=== CLASSIFICATION KEY ===
A - KEEP_REQUIRED_RUNTIME: application source, schemas, configuration, migrations, Docker/build inputs, or other files required to run the product.
B - KEEP_REQUIRED_TESTS: automated tests, fixtures, test configuration, or test harnesses used by the maintained validation surface.
C - KEEP_REQUIRED_REPRODUCIBILITY: tracked launchers, installers, audit/preflight scripts, reports, evidence bundles, source snapshots, or backups needed to reproduce competition work or explain prior changes.
D - REVIEW_REQUIRED: potentially obsolete, duplicated, historical, or untracked material where purpose, provenance, or dependency could not be proven from the read-only scan.
E - SAFE_TO_DELETE_GENERATED: reproducible generated/cache output with no source-of-truth role. Deletion is a recommendation only; no deletion was performed.
F - SAFE_TO_DELETE_DUPLICATE_OR_CONFIRMED_TEMPORARY: duplicate/temporary material whose disposable status is directly proven. No item was placed here during this audit because the available evidence was insufficient for a confident per-file determination.
